What it is

An endless 3D runner where you don't run — you swim. Living water carries you through the pipes under one huge city: waves rock you, currents pull, whirlpools drag you down. Steer with your finger, jump over trash, dive under pipes, and don't let yourself dissolve before you get home.

In the pipe

Fart turbo

Eat mini-poops, store gas, then hit the button. The more you saved, the longer the turbo burns.
